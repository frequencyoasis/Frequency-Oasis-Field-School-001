Reference card / core concepts

Numbers that help you reason

Sound propagation time

time (ms) ≈ distance (m) ÷ 343 × 1000

At ordinary room temperature, sound travels at approximately 343 metres per second. Actual speed changes with temperature and conditions. The readout estimates direct-path travel time; it does not model all arrivals.

Idealized axial room modes

fₙ = n × c ÷ (2L)

For an ideal rectangular room, the axial mode series along one dimension depends on sound speed, the dimension length, and integer mode order n. Real rooms have boundary losses, coupled modes, openings, furniture, and other complications. A single calculated frequency is not a complete diagnosis.

Inverse-square intuition

In a free field, doubling distance from a point-like source reduces sound pressure level by roughly 6 dB. Rooms are not free fields: reflections, source directivity, and boundaries can change the observed result.

Non-negotiable distinction

Illustration is not simulation.

This build has a perspective canvas, draggable position markers, derived geometry, approximate propagation time, and an idealized first axial-mode estimate. It does not solve full acoustic wave equations.

What a production release needs

  • Validated room-mode and boundary models
  • Frequency-dependent absorption and source directivity
  • Measured or validated impulse-response examples
  • Format-specific cinema and live-system references
  • Expert review, accessible controls, and practical scoring

Do not present simulated SPL, RT60, phase response, or certified placement compliance until those calculations are actually implemented and validated.

SELF-REPORTED PRACTICE
Mission protocol

Hypothesis → controlled change → evidence

Before each mission, write what you think is wrong. Change one variable. Listen or measure under controlled conditions. Record what changed and what did not.

Evidence checklist

  • Setup and equipment documented
  • One change isolated at a time
  • Listening level matched where appropriate
  • Measurement method named
  • Limitations and next test recorded
The checklist tracks your practice. It cannot independently verify that a real-world task was completed correctly.
